**Controller:** {{COMPANY}} ("Customer"). **Processor:** 拓域 TradeLeads platform operator ("Provider").

1. **Subject matter.** Provider processes personal data only to provide the B2B lead-generation, outreach, inbox and CRM service, on Customer's documented instructions.
2. **Categories.** Business contact data (name, title, business email/phone, company), inquiry/chat content, email correspondence. No special categories.
3. **Confidentiality.** Personnel are bound by confidentiality; access is role-based and logged (audit log). Support access requires Customer's time-limited grant.
4. **Security (Art. 32).** Tenant isolation, TLS in transit, encryption at rest for credentials, bank details and messaging identifiers (field-level Fernet), 2FA, password policy, daily encrypted backups (14-day retention), monitoring and alerting.
5. **Sub-processors.** Listed in the sub-processor list; Customer is notified 30 days before changes and may object.
6. **Assistance.** Provider offers DSR tooling (access, erasure, rectification, objection) and assists with DPIAs.
7. **Breach notification.** Provider notifies Customer without undue delay and no later than 48 hours after becoming aware, so Customer can meet its 72-hour obligation.
8. **Deletion.** On termination, after a 30-day grace period, all Customer data is deleted and a deletion certificate is issued; backups expire within 14 days.
9. **Audits.** Provider makes available information necessary to demonstrate compliance.
10. **Transfers.** Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2/3) apply to transfers outside the EEA.
